WalletCast

See your balance months ahead

coming soon to the App Store

Description

WalletCast shows how much money you’ll have, day by day, months ahead. Add your income, bills, subscriptions, installments and loans once, and it works out your balance for every day ahead, so you see the low points before payday and can plan around them.

There’s no bank connection: you enter what you know, and update your balance from your banking app now and then to keep the forecast on track.

swift · swiftui · swiftdata · cloudkit · ios

Forecast

  • Your expected balance today and the lowest point ahead
  • Monthly and daily charts, up to a year ahead
  • Every upcoming payment, day by day
  • Bills and income that change each month use their usual amount until you enter what you really paid

What If?

  • Try a purchase, a new monthly payment or extra income
  • See how it changes your lowest balance and your balance in a year, before you commit
  • Nothing you try there is saved

Spending

  • Spending by category, month by month
  • One-time payments, and expected everyday spending averaged from past months
  • Installments and loans that stop on their own after the last payment

Apple Intelligence

  • A short written outlook for your month, including how much you could set aside
  • Describe a payment in your own words, like “45 for groceries yesterday”, and WalletCast fills it in
  • Category suggestions as you type
  • Runs on your device; needs a device with Apple Intelligence and a supported language

Siri and Shortcuts

  • “What’s my balance in WalletCast”
  • “Add a payment in WalletCast”
  • Ask when your balance is lowest, or what’s due this week

Private

  • No sign-up, no bank logins, no ads, no tracking
  • Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud
  • Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID
  • Read the privacy policy

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26.2 or later. iCloud sync needs an iCloud account; Apple Intelligence features need a compatible device.

WalletCast is free. If it helps you, you can leave a tip in Settings.
